## 2. Foundational Business, Military & Strategy Literature

The framework also draws from seminal strategy, governance, and organizational management works:

1. **The OODA Loop (Observe, Orient, Decide, Act):**
   * **Creator:** Col. John Boyd (USAF)
   * **Reference:** *A Discourse on Winning and Losing* (1987). Used in our execution playbooks for high-uncertainty decision loops.
2. **Blue Ocean Strategy & The ERRC Matrix:**
   * **Authors:** W. Chan Kim and Renée Mauborgne
   * **Reference:** *Blue Ocean Strategy: How to Create Uncontested Market Space and Make the Competition Irrelevant* (Harvard Business Review Press, 2005). Adapted in our market strategy playbook.
3. **Single-Threaded Leadership & Type 1 / Type 2 Decisions:**
   * **Origin:** Amazon Leadership Principles & Jeff Bezos Shareholder Letters (1997–2016). Formulated into our Single-Threaded Ownership (STO) governance model.
4. **Product-Market Fit & The 40% Retention Rule:**
   * **Author:** Sean Ellis
   * **Reference:** *Hacking Growth* & The Sean Ellis PMF Test ("Very Disappointed" metric). Integrated into our founder-led sales and retention benchmarks.
5. **The Five Competitive Forces & Industry Structure:**
   * **Author:** Michael E. Porter
   * **Reference:** *Competitive Strategy: Techniques for Analyzing Industries and Competitors* (Free Press, 1980). Adapted for market entry barriers.
6. **Crossing the Chasm:**
   * **Author:** Geoffrey A. Moore
   * **Reference:** *Crossing the Chasm: Marketing and Selling Disruptive Products to Mainstream Customers* (HarperBusiness, 1991). Influencing our "Becoming Core" and beachhead transition models.
7. **One Page Project Manager (OPPM):**
   * **Author:** Clark A. Campbell
   * **Reference:** *The One-Page Project Manager* (John Wiley & Sons). Adapted in our "Think Big, Work Small" execution cadence.
8. **The Conative Index & Action Modes:**
   * **Author:** Kathy Kolbe
   * **Reference:** Kolbe Corp (Fact Finder, Follow Thru, Quick Start, Implementor). Referenced in founder operating profile evaluations.
9. **Founder Advisor Standard Template (FAST):**
   * **Origin:** Founder Institute (Adeo Ressi)
   * **Reference:** The FAST Agreement standard for early-stage advisor equity vesting and performance matrices.
