### 1. RECOGNITION OF COMPANY OWNERSHIP ("ZERO HOSTAGE CODE")
The Contributor hereby expressly acknowledges that all software code, scripts, repositories, database schemas, algorithms, user interface designs, trademarks, domain names, cloud infrastructure accounts (AWS, GCP, Azure), API keys, and business plans developed for or related to the Company's business are the sole and exclusive property of the Company.

**The Contributor agrees that under NO circumstances shall they:**
* Assert personal ownership over any GitHub/GitLab organization, repository, or commit history developed for the Company.
* Withhold, lock, encrypt, or refuse to surrender administrative credentials to any cloud provider, registrar, or SaaS account upon separation.
* Demand ransom, equity bonuses, or severance payments as a condition of transferring repository or domain ownership.

### 2. IRREVOCABLE ASSIGNMENT OF INVENTIONS
The Contributor hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Company all of their worldwide right, title, and interest in and to any and all:
* Inventions, software programs, architectures, improvements, discoveries, designs, and derivative works (whether patentable or not).
* Copyrights, trade secrets, trademarks, moral rights, and all other intellectual property rights.

This assignment applies to all Inventions authored, conceived, or reduced to practice by the Contributor, solely or jointly with others, from the inception of the Company's project forward.
