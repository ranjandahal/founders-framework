# Quarterly Founder Calibration Review (FCR) Worksheet
### The 90-Day Operational Rhythm for Performance Calibration, Milestone Verification & Radical Candor

> **How to Use:**  
> 1. Complete independently every 90 days before your quarterly executive offsite.  
> 2. Exchange scorecards with co-founders 24 hours in advance.  
> 3. Convene for a 2-hour calibration session focusing exclusively on score deltas $> 1.0$ point.  
> 4. Formally certify Tranche B milestone completions.
