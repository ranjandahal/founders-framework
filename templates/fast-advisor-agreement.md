# Founder Advisor Standard Agreement (FAST)
### Venture-Aligned Strategic Advisory Contract with Milestone Vesting & Performance Clawback

> **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)  
> **Purpose:** Engage strategic domain, technical, or commercial advisors without burning dead equity. Mandates active quarterly contributions and immediate termination for non-responsiveness.

---

This **ADVISORY AGREEMENT** (the "Agreement") is entered into as of **[Date]** (the "Effective Date"), by and between **[Company Name, LLC/Inc.]**, a [State of Incorporation] corporation (the "Company"), and **[Advisor Name]** ("Advisor").

### 1. Services & Scope of Advisory Engagement
Advisor agrees to provide high-impact strategic advisory services to the Company in the following designated focus areas (check all applicable):
- [ ] **Technical & Architectural Oversight:** System architecture review, security/compliance posture review, senior engineering hiring vetting.
- [ ] **Commercial & Strategic Introductions:** Facilitating warm introductions to prospective enterprise customers, payer networks, or distribution channels.
- [ ] **Fundraising & Investor Guidance:** Reviewing pitch narratives, assisting in data room preparation, introducing institutional seed/Series A venture capital firms.
- [ ] **Regulatory & Domain Navigation:** Guidance regarding state statutory requirements, compliance audits, or accreditation standards.

### 2. Time Commitment & Cadence
Advisor agrees to maintain the following regular operating cadence:
* **Monthly Strategic Briefing:** Participate in at least one (1) sixty-minute structured video briefing per calendar month with the founding team.
* **Ad-Hoc Tactical Access:** Reasonably available via email, Slack, or telephone for quick consultations (not to exceed 4 hours per month in total).
* **Quarterly Performance Review:** Participate in the Company's Quarterly Founder Calibration Review (FCR) to evaluate milestone achievement.

---

### 3. Equity Compensation & Vesting Schedule
In full consideration for the Services rendered, and subject to the Board of Directors’ approval, the Company shall grant Advisor **[Number]** Shares of Common Stock / Stock Options (the "Equity Grant"), representing approximately **[0.25% – 1.0%]** of the Company’s fully diluted capitalization as of the Effective Date.

#### 3.1 Vesting Terms (The 2-Year FAST Schedule)
* **Vesting Period:** Twenty-four (24) months total vesting duration.
* **The 3-Month Trial Cliff:** No shares shall vest until the three (3) month anniversary of the Effective Date. Upon the 3-month anniversary, 12.5% of the total Equity Grant shall vest, confirming mutual commitment.
* **Monthly Linear Vesting:** Following the 3-month cliff, the remaining 87.5% of the Equity Grant shall vest in equal monthly installments over the following twenty-one (21) months, **strictly conditioned upon active engagement**.

```
  [ Day 0: Effective Date ]
             |
  [ Month 3: 3-Month Trial Cliff (12.5% Vests) ]  <-- Mutual "No Hard Feelings" Exit Window
             |
  [ Months 4–24: Linear Monthly Vesting ]        <-- Active Quarterly Milestone Deliverables
```

---

### 4. Performance Milestones & The 60-Day Unresponsiveness Clawback

#### 4.1 Active Quarterly Contribution Standard
Advisor equity is not a gift for passive name-lending on a website. Continued vesting is contingent upon Advisor delivering at least **one (1) verifiable quarterly contribution**:
1. Introduction to at least two (2) qualified enterprise prospective customers.
2. Introduction to at least one (1) institutional venture capital lead.
3. Conducting a comprehensive quarterly technical, architectural, or regulatory review.

#### 4.2 The 60-Day Ghosting / Non-Responsiveness Clawback
If Advisor fails to attend scheduled monthly briefings or remains completely unresponsive to Company inquiries for a consecutive period of **sixty (60) calendar days**:
1. All vesting shall immediately and permanently cease as of the 60th day of unresponsiveness.
2. All unvested shares/options shall be forfeited back to the Company’s equity pool immediately.
3. The Company shall have the right (but not obligation) to repurchase any vested shares held by Advisor at fair market value (or nominal par value if pre-funded).

---

### 5. Confidentiality, Work Product & IP Assignment

#### 5.1 Confidential Information
Advisor agrees to hold in strict confidence all proprietary trade secrets, financial models, architecture designs, customer lists, and source code of the Company. Advisor shall not disclose such information to any third party without express written permission.

#### 5.2 Assignment of Work Product
To the extent Advisor authors, creates, or contributes to any documentation, architecture specifications, algorithms, or code on behalf of the Company, Advisor hereby **irrevocably assigns all right, title, and interest in and to such Work Product to the Company**.

---

### 6. Non-Exclusivity & Competing Engagements
The Company acknowledges that Advisor provides advisory services to other businesses. However, Advisor expressly covenants that during the term of this Agreement, Advisor shall not serve as an active founder, officer, director, or substantial advisor to any direct commercial competitor of the Company without prior written disclosure and consent.

---

### 7. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, upon **fourteen (14) days' written notice** to the other party. Upon termination, vesting ceases immediately.
