### ARTICLE 2: TWO-TRANCHE HYBRID VESTING

2.1 **Hybrid Vesting Structure:**  
To align incentives with sustained commitment and verified product delivery, each Founder's equity grant shall be divided into **Tranche A (Time-Based Runway)** and **Tranche B (Milestone-Gated Performance)**:

* **Tranche A (Time-Based, 60% of Grant):**  
  Vests over a forty-eight (48) month period commencing on [Start Date], subject to a twelve (12) month cliff:
  - If a Founder separates prior to twelve (12) months, zero percent (0%) of Tranche A shall vest.
  - On the first anniversary of the Start Date, twenty-five percent (25%) of Tranche A shall vest immediately.
  - The remaining seventy-five percent (75%) shall vest in equal monthly installments over the subsequent thirty-six (36) months.

* **Tranche B (Milestone-Gated, 40% of Grant):**  
  Vests strictly upon verifiable completion of the following objective corporate milestones:
  - **Milestone 1 (10%):** Production launch of v1.0 core architecture with passing automated security and compliance scans.
  - **Milestone 2 (10%):** Execution of first three (3) paying commercial customer contracts.
  - **Milestone 3 (10%):** Rollout of secondary product suites or integrations with active weekly usage.
  - **Milestone 4 (10%):** Closing of institutional financing ($\ge\$500,000$) or reaching $\$250,000$ ARR run-rate.

2.2 **Acceleration (Double-Trigger):**  
In the event of a Change of Control (merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets), unvested shares shall accelerate **only** if a Founder is terminated without Cause or constructively dismissed within twelve (12) months following the closing.

2.3 **Section 83(b) Tax Election:**  
Each Founder covenants and agrees to timely file an IRS Section 83(b) election within thirty (30) days of the grant date and submit proof of filing to corporate records.

### ARTICLE 3: SINGLE-THREADED OWNERSHIP (STO) & DECISION RIGHTS

3.1 **Single-Threaded Ownership:**  
The Founders reject consensus management. Each operational vertical shall have exactly one accountable Founder with unilateral decision rights:
* **Architecture & Infrastructure:** [CTO Name] has sole authority over tech stack, database models, cloud providers, and code deployments.
* **Commercial & Capital:** [CEO Name] has sole authority over pricing, sales contracts, legal counsel, and investor negotiations.
* **Operations & Field Compliance:** [COO Name] has sole authority over regulatory audits, state filings, and direct operations.

3.2 **Type 1 vs. Type 2 Decisions:**  
* **Type 1 Decisions (Irreversible):** Mergers, entity dissolution, equity grants, and incurring debt $>\$10,000$ require unanimous or majority Board approval.
* **Type 2 Decisions (Reversible):** Operational decisions within an STO vertical shall be decided unilaterally by the designated Founder within twenty-four (24) hours without committee approval.

3.3 **Review SLA:**  
Founders commit to reviewing pull requests, legal documents, and architecture RFCs within twenty-four (24) business hours.

---

### ARTICLE 4: DEPARTURE & LEAVER PROVISIONS

4.1 **Bad Leaver Provisions:**  
If a Founder is terminated for Cause (fraud, embezzlement, felony, willful breach of fiduciary duty, or abandonment of duties), all unvested shares forfeit immediately, and the Company shall have the irrevocable right to repurchase all vested shares at the original nominal purchase price ($0.0001 per share).

4.2 **Good Leaver Provisions:**  
If a Founder separates due to death, permanent disability, or mutual amicable separation approved by the Board, the departing Founder shall retain all vested Tranche A shares. All unvested Tranche A and unearned Tranche B shares shall revert to the Company treasury.

4.3 **Right of First Refusal (ROFR):**  
No Founder may sell, transfer, or pledge any shares to any third party without first offering the Company and remaining Founders the right to purchase the shares on identical terms.
