# B2B SaaS Customer Pilot Agreement & Term Sheet
### Venture-Grade Commercial Framework with Quantitative Success Criteria & Auto-Conversion

> **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)  
> **Purpose:** Protect early-stage software companies from "endless free pilot" syndrome. Establishes paid evaluation parameters, bounded time limits, explicit success criteria, and automatic conversion into an annual Master Services Agreement (MSA).

---

This **PILOT EVALUATION AGREEMENT** (the "Agreement") is entered into as of **[Date]** (the "Effective Date"), by and between **[Startup Company Name, LLC/Inc.]** ("Provider"), and **[Customer Agency/Company Name]** ("Customer").

### 1. Pilot Purpose & Scope of Access
Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to access and evaluate Provider’s proprietary software platform, **[Product Name]** (the "Platform"), for the internal evaluation period defined herein.

* **Authorized Scope:** Access for up to **[Number]** authorized administrative users and **[Number]** frontline staff/caregivers.
* **Pilot Term:** **[30 or 60]** calendar days, commencing on **[Start Date]** and concluding on **[End Date]** (the "Pilot Period").

---

### 2. Pilot Pricing & Commercial Commitment

#### 2.1 Pilot Setup & Evaluation Fee
Customer shall pay Provider a non-refundable evaluation fee of **$[Amount, e.g., $1,500 – $5,000]** upon execution of this Agreement (the "Pilot Fee"). 

> **Venture Best Practice (The Credit Mechanism):**  
> 100% of the Pilot Fee paid by Customer shall be credited toward the Customer’s first-year annual subscription fee upon conversion.

#### 2.2 Annual Master Services Subscription Terms (Upon Conversion)
If the Platform meets the agreed-upon Success Criteria outlined in Section 3, Customer agrees to convert to an annual subscription under the following commercial parameters:
* **Annual Contract Value (ACV):** **$[Annual Amount]** per year, billed [Annually upfront / Quarterly].
* **Subscription Tier / Seat Package:** [e.g., Enterprise Core Tier: up to 50 active clients/beds, unlimited staff].
* **Standard Contract Duration:** Twelve (12) months from Pilot conclusion, renewing automatically.

---

### 3. Mutual Success Criteria & Automatic Conversion Mechanism

#### 3.1 Quantitative Success Criteria
The Pilot shall be deemed successful if the Platform satisfies the following objective, verifiable benchmarks during the Pilot Period:
1. **Operational Time Savings:** Frontline staff or coordinators achieve at least **[e.g., 50%]** reduction in manual reconciliation/documentation time.
2. **System Adoption:** At least **[e.g., 80%]** of onboarded pilot users successfully complete their daily shift documentation/logs within the Platform.
3. **Platform Reliability:** The Platform maintains **$\ge 99.5\%$** uptime during standard business hours.
4. **Compliance & Reporting Output:** Platform generates compliant state/audit reports verified by Customer’s Director of Compliance/Operations.

```
 [ Day 0: Pilot Kickoff & White-Glove Ingestion ]
                    |
 [ Days 1–23: Active Workflow Execution & Telemetry Tracking ]
                    |
 [ Day 24: Formal Success Criteria Review Session ]
                    |
   Did Platform satisfy Section 3.1 Criteria?
         /                      \
      [ YES ]                 [ NO ]
         |                      |
 [ Auto-Converts to       [ Graceful Offboarding;
   Annual MSA on Day 30 ]   Full Data Export Provided ]
```

#### 3.2 The Auto-Conversion Covenant
If the Platform satisfies the Success Criteria, and Customer does not provide written notice of termination citing specific unmet criteria at least **seven (7) calendar days prior to the expiration of the Pilot Period**, this Agreement shall **automatically convert into an Annual Master Services Agreement** under the terms set forth in Section 2.2.

---

### 4. Data Security, Privacy & Compliance (HIPAA / SOC 2)
1. **Customer Data Ownership:** All data, records, documents, and inputs provided by Customer remain the exclusive property of Customer.
2. **Confidentiality & BAA:** If Customer operates in healthcare or processes Protected Health Information (PHI), Provider and Customer shall simultaneously execute Provider's standard **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)** attached as Exhibit A.
3. **Auditability & Export:** Upon termination of the Pilot for any reason, Provider shall provide Customer with a complete, structured export of all Customer Data within five (5) business days and subsequently purge Customer Data from staging environments in accordance with statutory retention laws.

---

### 5. Provider Support & White-Glove Onboarding
Provider commits to delivering executive-level support during the Pilot Period:
* Dedicated Slack/Teams or priority email channel with a **$< 2$-hour response SLA**.
* Two (2) scheduled 45-minute training workshops for Customer administrative staff and managers.
* Direct concierge assistance with initial data ingestion, template configurations, and user provisioning.
